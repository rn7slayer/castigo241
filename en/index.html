Castigo241

A group of Brazilian teenage hackers that attacked the infrastructure of child pornography sellers. The targets were Discord servers, Telegram channels and websites of registered companies used as storefronts.

Operations

  • 37 Discord servers taken down
  • 2 Telegram servers taken down
  • 1 company website taken down

Halt

Operations were suspended by government order. The reason was the safety of the group itself, made up only of minors.
